My 3D Needle Felting Project

A closer look at my creative process and finished sculpture.

From Wool to Sculpture

For this project, I explored how loose wool fibres can be shaped into a three-dimensional piece of art using needle felting techniques.

I focused on developing the shape, texture, proportions and details of my sculpture. This project encouraged me to experiment with materials, solve creative challenges and develop my artistic skills.

Medium: Wool and felting materials

Technique: 3D needle felting

Focus: Form, texture and detail

My Method

The Creative Process

Three stages that help turn an idea into a 3D artwork.

01

Plan & Design

I developed my idea, considered the final shape and selected the colours and wool needed for the sculpture.

02

Build & Shape

I layered and shaped the wool, using repeated felting motions to gradually create a firm three-dimensional form.

03

Refine & Present

I refined the surface, adjusted the details and prepared the sculpture for its final presentation.

Artist Statement

What I Learned

"Art is about transforming simple materials into something meaningful through imagination, patience and experimentation."

This project helped me explore a different form of sculpture and understand how texture and shape can bring an idea to life. I learned the importance of patience, attention to detail and learning from mistakes throughout the creative process.
